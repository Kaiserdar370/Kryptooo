Market glossary

32 terms used across kryptooo, defined in a sentence or two.

#ABCDEFHILMPRSTVW
52-week range
The highest and lowest prices of a security over the past year.
Ask
The lowest price at which a seller is currently willing to sell a security.
Bear market
A prolonged decline in prices, commonly defined as a fall of 20% or more from a recent peak.
Bid
The highest price at which a buyer is currently willing to buy a security.
Bull market
A prolonged period of rising prices and generally improving investor confidence.
Circulating supply
The number of coins or tokens currently available to the public. Used to calculate a cryptoasset's market cap.
Close
The final trading price of a security in a session. The previous close is the baseline for the daily change.
Cryptocurrency
A digital asset that uses cryptography and a distributed ledger to record ownership and transfers.
Dividend
A distribution of a portion of a company's profits to its shareholders, usually paid in cash.
Dominance
An asset's share of total cryptocurrency market cap. Bitcoin dominance is the most widely followed.
ETF
Exchange-traded fund. A fund that holds a basket of assets and trades on an exchange like a single stock.
Fear and greed index
A 0 to 100 gauge of market sentiment, where low values signal fear and high values signal greed.
Float
The shares of a company that are available for public trading, excluding those held by insiders or governments.
Fully diluted valuation
Price multiplied by the maximum possible supply of a token. Shows implied size if all coins were issued.
Heatmap
A visual of many assets at once, with tile size showing market cap and color showing price change.
Index
A rules-based measure of a group of securities, such as the S&P 500. It cannot be bought directly.
Limit order
An instruction to buy or sell only at a specified price or better.
Liquidity
How easily an asset can be bought or sold without moving its price. Tight spreads and high volume indicate high liquidity.
Market capitalization
The total market value of a company or cryptoasset: price multiplied by shares or coins outstanding.
Market order
An instruction to buy or sell immediately at the best available price.
Momentum
The tendency of recent price direction to continue. Often measured over days or months.
P/E ratio
Price-to-earnings ratio. The share price divided by earnings per share, used to compare valuations.
Previous close
The closing price from the last completed session.
Rebalancing
Adjusting a portfolio back to its target allocation after price changes have shifted the weights.
Sector
A group of companies in the same broad industry, such as technology or energy.
Spread
The difference between the bid and the ask. A narrower spread usually means a more liquid market.
Stablecoin
A cryptoasset designed to hold a steady value, typically pegged to a currency such as the US dollar.
Ticker symbol
A short code that identifies a security, such as AAPL for Apple or BTC for Bitcoin.
Total return
Performance including both price changes and reinvested dividends or distributions.
Volatility
The degree to which an asset's price fluctuates over time. Higher volatility means larger swings.
Volume
The number of shares, contracts or coins traded in a period.
Watchlist
A personal list of securities you want to track without owning them.
