Who we are

This policy applies to the website at www.kryptooo.example, operated by kryptooo ("kryptooo", "we", "us"). You can reach us about privacy at privacy@kryptooo.example.

Information we collect

Information you provide

You do not need an account to use kryptooo. If you email us, we receive your email address and whatever you include in your message. We use it only to respond and to keep a record of the conversation.

Information collected automatically

When you visit, our hosting provider and our servers may log technical data such as your IP address, browser type, device type, pages requested, referring page, and date and time. This is standard for operating and securing a website.

Information stored on your device

We store a small amount of data in your browser: your chosen theme (light or dark) and your cookie choice. Your watchlist is kept only in your browser's memory while the page is open, and it is not sent to us.

How we use information

  • To operate, maintain and secure the site.
  • To respond to your messages and requests.
  • To understand, in aggregate, how the site is used so we can improve it.
  • To show advertising that supports the site, as described below.
  • To comply with legal obligations.

Legal bases (EEA, UK and Switzerland)

Where data protection law requires a legal basis, we rely on consent for advertising cookies and similar technologies, legitimate interests for security and site improvement, and legal obligation where applicable. You can withdraw consent at any time using "Cookie preferences" in the footer.

Advertising and Google

We may display ads served by third parties, including Google AdSense. Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on your prior visits to this and other websites.

  • Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to you based on your visit to our site and/or other sites on the internet.
  • You can opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings. You can also opt out of some third-party vendors' use of cookies for personalized advertising at aboutads.info.
  • To learn how Google uses data from sites that use its services, see How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services.

We load advertising scripts only after you accept advertising cookies. If you choose "Essential only", ad scripts are not loaded.

Cookies

Our Cookie Policy lists the cookies and storage we and our partners use, and how to control them.

Sharing of information

We do not sell your personal information for money. We share information only with: service providers that host and secure the site; advertising partners, if you consent to advertising cookies; and authorities, when required by law or to protect rights and safety.

International transfers

Our providers may process data in countries other than your own. Where required, we use safeguards such as standard contractual clauses to protect transferred data.

Retention

We keep server logs for a limited period needed for security and operations, and correspondence for as long as needed to handle your request and meet legal obligations. Browser-stored settings remain until you clear them.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, delete or export your personal data, to object to or restrict certain processing, and to withdraw consent. Residents of California may also have the right to know what personal information is collected and to opt out of its "sale" or "sharing" for cross-context behavioral advertising. To exercise a right, email privacy@kryptooo.example. You may also lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority.

Children

kryptooo is not directed to children under 16, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has sent us personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures, including encrypted connections, to protect information. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure.

External links

Our pages may link to other websites. We are not responsible for their privacy practices, and we encourage you to read their policies.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The "Last updated" date above shows when it last changed. Material changes will be highlighted on the site.

Contact

Privacy questions: privacy@kryptooo.example.
